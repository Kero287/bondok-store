Placing your order

Choose your pieces, open your bag, and enter your contact and delivery details at checkout.

  1. Choose card payment through Paymob when available, or cash on delivery with shipping plus a 50 EGP deposit paid upfront.
  2. Complete card payment on Paymob, or transfer to 01275446186 and upload your receipt for verification.
  3. After payment confirmation, your order arrives within 48 working hours. For cash on delivery, the 50 EGP deposit is deducted from the product price; pay only the remaining product balance on delivery.

Keeping up with your order

For an update, message our team on WhatsApp with your order number and the phone number used at checkout.

Keep your order reference and deposit receipt handy so we can find your purchase quickly.
